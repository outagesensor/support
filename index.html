OutageSensor

Know the moment the power goes out — and the moment it's back.

OutageSensor pairs with a small sensor at your home, cabin, or business to watch for power and internet outages around the clock. The moment something changes, you get a push notification — even if you're a thousand miles away.

Tap the + button on the dashboard, then follow the in-app setup flow: your phone connects to the sensor's temporary Wi-Fi hotspot, you choose your home network and give the sensor a name, and it's online within a minute or two. No soldering or extra hardware app needed — just the sensor and your phone.

A power outage means the sensor itself lost electricity — it uses the last moment of stored power to send one final message before going dark. An internet outage means the sensor still has power, but it can't reach your Wi-Fi or the internet, usually because your router or ISP is down rather than the electricity itself. OutageSensor tells these apart automatically, so you're not alerted about a real outage when it's really just a router hiccup.

Open the sensor's detail screen and tap Share Sensor to generate a QR code and a link. A family member or co-worker scans the code (or opens the link) in their own copy of the app to get read-only access — they'll see its live status and outage history and get their own outage notifications, but can't change its Wi-Fi setup or other settings. You can see everyone you've shared with, and revoke access at any time, from Manage Sharing. Handy for a shared vacation home or keeping an eye on a parent's house.

There's also a simpler option, Add Sensor by MAC Address, where anyone who knows the sensor's device ID can add it themselves for read-only monitoring — but unlike Share Sensor, this can't be revoked later, so it's best for cases where you fully trust whoever you give the ID to.
