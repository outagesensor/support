Delete Your Data

App: OutageSensor · Developer: Oleksandr Seminov

OutageSensor has no user accounts — there is no login. You can delete the data associated with your use of the app at any time, directly in the app, using the controls below.

Delete a sensor's outage history

  1. Open the OutageSensor app and tap the sensor.
  2. Tap Reset Stats.
  3. This permanently erases that sensor's stored outage history and statistics from our servers.

Remove a sensor completely

  1. Open the sensor's detail screen and tap Remove Sensor.
  2. This removes the sensor and deletes its registration, outage history, and the push-notification token associated with it. If the sensor was added via Share Sensor, choose Remove from App Only, which unlinks it from your copy of the app.
  3. Removed sensor data is permanently purged from our servers within 7 days.

What data this affects

The only data tied to your use of the app is: your sensors' outage history and events, the sensor names and share labels you choose, and a push-notification token used to send you outage alerts. All of it is removed by the steps above. We do not collect your name, email, account credentials, or location.

Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, or want to confirm deletion of any remaining data associated with your device tokens, email us at outage.sensor.support@gmail.com and we will process your request.
